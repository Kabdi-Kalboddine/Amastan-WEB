DMCA and illegal content reports

How to report copyright infringement or illegal content related to the Amastan website or Community.

Content on this website

For material hosted on amastansolution.com, email the notice address with all of the following:

  • Your contact name, address, telephone number, and email
  • Description of the copyrighted work you own
  • The URL of the allegedly infringing material
  • A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorized
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate and that you are authorized to act
  • Your physical or electronic signature

Content in Amastan Community

Community threads live in the Amastan customer and partner portals. Report copyright or illegal community content using the notice address below. Include the portal URL of the material when you have it.

Illegal content

To report unlawful content other than copyright, email staff with the URL, a short description, and why it is unlawful. Do not attach malware, packet captures, or secrets in the report.

Counter-notice

If your material was removed and you believe that was a mistake, email a counter-notice with your contact details, identification of the removed material, a statement under penalty of perjury of good-faith belief, consent to jurisdiction, and your signature.
