What this is

Amastan Community is Q&A for product questions, air-gapped / license operations, and feature requests. Threads live in the signed-in Amastan SaaS portal (customers and partners). This marketing site publishes the rules and legal pages only. It does not host posts.

This website is the rulebook

Anyone may read the rules here. Asking and answering requires a signed-in customer or partner account. There is no anonymous posting and no email-to-post on this site.

Text, Markdown, and code only

Amastan Community does not offer attachments or images. Do not post screenshots of appliances, packet captures, internal URLs, personal data, or license keys. Do not share DLP regular expressions — ever.

What this is not

  • Not a public internet forum and not GitHub
  • Not a cloud Secure Web Gateway
  • Not appliance SSO
  • Not a place to paste production config or secrets

How to participate

Accept the terms first. Then sign in to the Amastan customer or partner portal — we do not embed a post editor on this website.

1Read the Community terms. Posting in the portal means you accept them.
2Sign in to the customer or partner portal Amastan issued you.
3Write in text, Markdown, and code only. Do not attach images as a matter of Amastan policy.
4Use a product category: Product, Air-gap / license, or Feature request. Announcements are staff-only.

Need an account? Request a pilot. Existing customers and partners use the portal they already have.

Never post

If it would help an attacker or expose a customer environment, do not paste it. This list is mandatory on Community and in the terms.

  • Appliance tokens
  • HMAC intake keys
  • Stripe IDs
  • Internal IP addresses
  • Packet captures (pcaps)
  • DLP regular expressions (never share, including “examples”)
  • Policy plaintext
  • Employee directories
  • RBI sessions
  • Prompts or completions
  • Other-tenant data
  • Embargoed vulnerabilities
  • Screenshots of appliances, pcaps, URLs, personal data, or license keys

Terms, license, and reports

You must accept the Community terms (including the content license) before your first post. Copyright and illegal-content reports: use the DMCA page. Privacy requests: email staff.
